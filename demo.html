How it works

  1. Load 12 months of EUROCONTROL ADR data (~2.1M departing flights).
  2. Engineer 35 schedule-based features — flight attributes, timing, congestion, and month-by-month historical statistics.
  3. Train a global CatBoost model plus 6 airport-specific models (Rome, London, Istanbul, Amsterdam, Paris, Frankfurt) on Kaggle GPU.
  4. Blend 70/30 between airport and global models at the six hubs; global model everywhere else.
  5. Validate with expanding-time folds, predict the held-out test set, and submit — the leaderboard keeps each team's best of 5 submissions.
